Privacy / Captionlocal

Subtitle Editor for SRT & VTT Files

Effective
12 November 2026
Applies to
Version 1.0.0
Listing
On the tools page

Edit and re-time SRT and WebVTT subtitle files against a local video.

What it can access

  • storage

    Reads the administrator policy (chrome.storage.managed) and saves your preferences on this device (storage.local, never sync). The extension requests no host permissions, no tabs access and no downloads access. The editor opens in its own tab when you click the toolbar icon; the subtitle and video files you choose are read through the file picker or drag and drop.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

Subtitle files and videos you open are read on your device and never uploaded; the extension makes no network requests and collects no data. Unsaved drafts (the subtitle text, its file name and a timestamp) are kept in this browser's local storage (IndexedDB) until you save or discard them. Preferences are kept in chrome.storage.local. The editor's trust line says "Permissions: storage" read from the live manifest.

What is stored

What it stores, on your device only: your preferences (limits, theme, defaults) in Chrome's local extension storage (never synced), and, until you save or discard them, drafts of subtitle files you are editing in this browser's local storage so a closed tab does not lose your work. A draft holds the subtitle text, its file name and a timestamp; it is deleted when you save or discard it.

More detail

The editor reads the subtitle files and videos you choose, on your device, to let you edit them. They are never uploaded, and the extension makes no network requests.

There is no analytics, no telemetry, no account and no advertising.
