Clipboard History: Private, Auto-Clearing — Privacy

Effective
12 November 2026
Applies to
Version 1.0.0
Listing
On the tools page

Keep a searchable history of the text you copy, on this device only.

What it can access

  • clipboardRead

    Reads text you copy, in Chrome or other apps while Chrome is running, so it can be listed in your history. Text is kept in memory (or encrypted if you set a passcode) and never sent anywhere. Install warning: "Read data you copy and paste". This is the single purpose.

  • offscreen

    A hidden extension page that reads the clipboard in the background; Chrome only allows clipboard reads from a page, not from the service worker. Reason: CLIPBOARD. No install warning.

  • storage

    Saves your settings, pinned items and the in-memory history. No install warning.

  • alarms

    Removes expired items from disk every 15 minutes and locks the popup after the idle time you chose. No install warning.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

The text you copy is handled on your device only. Nothing is transferred off the device.

What is stored

Where it keeps it: recent copies in Chrome's in-memory extension storage (gone when Chrome closes); pins in Chrome's local extension storage, scrambled with a key stored beside them, or encrypted with your passcode if you set one. With a passcode and "Keep history across restarts", recent copies are also kept in local extension storage, encrypted, and deleted after the time you choose (1 hour to 7 days).

How to remove it: Settings, then Your data, then Delete everything; or remove the extension, which deletes everything it stored.

More detail

Clipboard History reads the text you copy and stores it only on your device: in memory by default, or encrypted with your passcode if you choose to keep history across restarts. It does not send anything anywhere, has no account, no analytics, and makes no network requests. Text that looks like a card number, key, one-time code or password is not stored.

What it reads: text on the system clipboard, in Chrome or any other app, while Chrome is running and capture is on. It does not read web pages, and it cannot tell which site or app a copy came from.
