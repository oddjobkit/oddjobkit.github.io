Remove EXIF Data & GPS from Photos — Privacy

Effective
12 November 2026
Applies to
Version 1.0.0
Listing
On the tools page

Remove location, camera, date, author and other hidden metadata from JPEG, PNG and WebP photos on the user's own device, without re-encoding the image.

What it can access

  • storage

    Reads the administrator policy (chrome.storage.managed) and saves the on/off options and the file-name suffix on this device. The extension requests no host permissions, no tabs access and no downloads access; files you add are read through the file picker or drag and drop and saved through the normal browser download.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

Photos you add are read and rewritten entirely on your device. The extension makes no network requests, collects no data and has no analytics; its settings stay on your device or come from your organisation's policy.

What is stored

It stores four settings (keep orientation, keep colour profile, keep Content Credentials, file-name suffix) in your browser's extension storage on your device.

More detail

The extension reads photos you choose (file picker, drag and drop, paste) and rewrites them in your browser. Photos, their metadata and the results never leave your device: the extension makes no network requests and has no analytics, telemetry or account. It stores four settings (keep orientation, keep colour profile, keep Content Credentials, file-name suffix) in your browser's extension storage on your device. If your organisation sets a managed policy for the extension, the extension reads it. Nothing is sold or shared.
