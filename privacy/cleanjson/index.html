Privacy / Cleanjson

JSON Formatter & Viewer (Local)

Effective
12 November 2026
Applies to
Version 1.0.0
Listing
On the tools page

Show JSON (the response you are looking at, a pasted snippet, or a file) as a collapsible, searchable tree, with no access to any site you have not chosen.

What it can access

  • activeTab

    Lets the toolbar button or shortcut format the JSON page you are on, only after you click.

  • scripting

    Runs the bundled viewer in that tab (and on sites you allow); no remote code.

  • storage

    Saves display settings and the list of sites you allowed, on this device (storage.local, never sync).

  • http://*/*, https://*/*Optional site access

    Requested one site at a time, only when you press "Always format JSON here"; each grant is listed in Settings and removable. Nothing is requested at install.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

cleanjson processes JSON entirely in your browser; it does not send, store or share page content, JSON, URLs or browsing history, and it makes no network requests. The only thing it stores, on your device, is your display settings and the list of sites you allowed. The popup's trust line shows "Permissions: activeTab, scripting, storage · Allowed sites: N. No network" from the live manifest.

What is stored

What it stores, on your device only (Chrome's local extension storage, never synced): your display settings (theme, font, depth and similar) and the list of sites you chose to allow. Chrome itself holds the permission for each allowed site; you can see and remove them in the extension's Settings or at chrome://extensions.

More detail

cleanjson processes JSON entirely in your browser. It does not send, store or share page content, JSON, URLs or browsing history, and it makes no network requests.

What it reads: only the document in a tab you asked it to format, or on a site you allowed, and only when that document is JSON. It reads it to draw the tree and discards it when the tab closes.

There is no analytics, no telemetry, no account and no advertising.
