HEIC to JPG Converter: WebP, AVIF to JPG/PNG — Privacy

Effective
12 November 2026
Applies to

Listing
On the tools page

Convert HEIC, WebP, AVIF and other images you already have to JPG, PNG or WebP, on your device.

What it can access

  • No permissions

    This extension requests no permissions. The toolbar button opens a tab; images are read from files you drop, paste or pick, converted inside that tab, and saved through your browser.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

Images are read from files the user drops, pastes or picks, converted inside the extension's own tab (in background workers), and saved back through the browser. The extension makes no network requests, has no analytics, and stores only the user's settings (output format, quality, fill colour, and whether the one-time rating link was shown) in the page's local storage. The trust line on the page says "No permissions · Nothing leaves your device".

What is stored

It makes no network requests, collects no data, has no analytics, and stores only your settings (output format, quality, fill colour) in your browser's local storage.

More detail

HEIC to JPG Converter processes your images entirely on your device. It makes no network requests, collects no data, has no analytics, and stores only your settings (output format, quality, fill colour) in your browser's local storage. The extension requests no permissions. Its pages are allowed to connect only to the extension itself, so it cannot send your files anywhere. Converted files carry no camera, date or location data.
