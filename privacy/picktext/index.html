Privacy / Picktext

Image to Text OCR – Copy Text from Images

Effective
13 November 2026
Applies to
Version 1.0.0
Listing
On the tools page

Copy the text out of any image or screenshot: select an area of the tab you are viewing, or paste, drop or choose a picture, edit the recognised text and copy it, all on your own computer.

What it can access

  • activeTab

    Lets you capture the tab you are looking at when you press the toolbar button or the shortcut; nothing is read in the background.

  • storage

    Remembers your language, reading order and display choices, applies administrator policy, and keeps the picture in view and its text in the browser's memory (session storage) so closing the tab loses nothing.

  • clipboardWriteOptional

    Requested only if you turn on "Copy automatically when done" in Settings. Copying with the Copy text button needs no permission.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

Website content, handled on this device only; never transmitted, sold or used for anything but the tool's one job.

What is stored

What it stores: your preferences (language, reading order, line joining, monospace font, automatic copy) in the browser's local extension storage on this device. The picture in view and its text are kept in the browser's memory (extension session storage, never written to disk) so closing the tab loses nothing; Start over or quitting Chrome clears them. There is no history.

More detail

Image to Text OCR processes the screenshots and images you give it on your device with bundled recognition models. Images, text and settings are never sent anywhere; the extension contains no analytics and cannot make network requests.

What it reads: only the picture you capture from the tab you pressed the toolbar button on (or pressed the shortcut for), paste, drop or choose. It never reads tabs in the background and has no access to websites.

What it shares: nothing, with no one.

Clipboard: text is copied only when you press Copy text, or after you turn on "Copy automatically when done" in Settings, which asks your browser for clipboard permission.

Administrators: managed policy can preset the language, the reading order, hide automatic copy, and lower the largest image size.
