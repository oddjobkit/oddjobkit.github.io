Image to Text OCR – Copy Text from Images — Privacy

Effective
12 November 2026
Applies to
Version 1.0.0
Listing
On the tools page

Copy the text out of any image or screenshot: select an area of the tab you are viewing, or paste, drop or choose a picture, edit the recognised text and copy it, all on your own computer.

What it can access

  • activeTab

    Lets you capture the tab you are looking at when you click the toolbar button or press the shortcut; nothing is read in the background.

  • storage

    Remembers your language, reading order and display choices, and applies administrator policy.

  • clipboardWriteOptional

    Requested only if you turn on "Copy automatically when done". Copying with the Copy button needs no permission.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

The picture you capture, paste, drop or choose is decoded and read on your device by bundled recognition models inside the extension's own page. The recognised text is shown in a text box and goes to the clipboard or a file only when you press Copy or Save (or after you turn on automatic copying). Settings (language, reading order, joined lines, monospace, automatic copy) are stored in storage.local on this device. Nothing is stored about your pictures or text, there is no history, and nothing is transmitted: the page's CSP is connect-src 'self', so the browser itself refuses any other connection. The page's trust line says "Permissions: activeTab, storage · Images, text and settings stay on this device."

What is stored

What it stores: your preferences (language, reading order, line joining, monospace font, automatic copy) in the browser's local extension storage on this device. It stores no pictures, no recognised text and no history.

More detail

Image to Text OCR processes the screenshots and images you give it on your device with bundled recognition models. Images, text and settings are never sent anywhere; the extension contains no analytics and cannot make network requests.

What it reads: only the picture you capture from the tab you clicked the toolbar button on (or pressed the shortcut for), paste, drop or choose. It never reads tabs in the background and has no access to websites.

What it shares: nothing, with no one.

Clipboard: text is copied only when you press Copy, or after you turn on "Copy automatically when done", which asks your browser for clipboard permission.

Administrators: managed policy can preset the language, the reading order, hide automatic copy, and lower the largest image size.
