Privacy / Cookiemute

Auto Reject Cookie Banners

Effective
13 November 2026
Applies to
Version 1.0.0
Listing
On the tools page

Automatically reject cookie consent banners on websites.

What it can access

  • http://*/*, https://*/* (host permissions, and the content script's matches, all frames, at document start)Site access

    Cookie banners are part of each website's own page, so the extension must run on every page and frame to find them. It only looks for the banner's buttons and clicks Reject. It sends nothing anywhere.

  • scripting

    Calls a banner's own "decline all" function inside the page for consent platforms that provide one.

  • storage

    Saves your settings, the sites you paused, and a count of banners handled.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

Auto Reject Cookie Banners runs on every page you open to find cookie banners and click Reject. It stores only your settings, the sites you paused, and a count of banners handled, on your device. It does not collect, send or share your browsing data and makes no network requests. Nothing leaves the device. Banner rules are bundled with the extension and change only when the extension is updated.

What is stored

It stores on your device: your settings (on/off, hide banners that offer no reject option, recognise unknown banners, the sites you paused) and a count of banners handled. Per open tab it keeps a short note (site name, which consent platform it found, what happened) until the tab or the browser closes.

More detail

This extension runs on every page you open to find cookie banners and click Reject. It reads the page on your device only, to do that, and does not store or send it.

It does not collect, send or share your browsing data, makes no network requests, has no account, and contains no analytics or advertising. Banner rules are bundled in the extension and change only when the extension is updated.

The "Report" link opens a page on GitHub in your browser with the site's name filled in; you choose whether to send it. GitHub's own terms apply to that page.
