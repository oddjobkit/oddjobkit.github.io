Privacy / Hexpick

Contrast checker + color picker (WCAG)

Effective
13 November 2026
Applies to
Version 1.0.0
Listing
On the tools page

Measure the WCAG 2.2 contrast between two colors you pick from your screen or an image, or type, and show a passing fix.

What it can access

  • No permissions

    The extension requests no permissions and no host access. It reads no web page. The browser's eyedropper samples a pixel only when the user selects it, and image sampling reads only an image the user pastes, drops or chooses in the popup.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

The colors you pick, type or sample are handled on this device only; never transmitted, sold or used for anything but the tool's one job.

What is stored

What is stored on your device: your settings (target level, recent-pairs options, single-key shortcuts), your current color pair, and your recent color pairs (up to 50; you can turn this off and delete them in Settings). Removing the extension deletes all of it.

More detail

Nothing leaves this device. The colors you pick, type or sample are handled on this device only; never transmitted, sold or used for anything but the tool's one job. They are processed in your browser and stored only in local extension storage on your device. The extension has no accounts, no analytics and makes no network requests. It requests no permissions and has no access to any website.

What is never collected or sent: the colors you check, the pages you visit, images you load, anything you copy.
