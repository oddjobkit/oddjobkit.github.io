Privacy / Contrast Checker + Color Picker (WCAG)

Contrast Checker + Color Picker (WCAG) — Privacy

Effective
12 November 2026
Applies to
Version 1.0.0
Listing
On the tools page

Measure the WCAG 2.2 contrast between two colors you pick from your screen or an image, or type, and show a passing fix.

What it can access

  • No permissions

    The extension requests no permissions and no host access. It reads no web page. The browser's eyedropper samples a pixel only when the user clicks it, and image sampling reads only an image the user pastes, drops or chooses in the popup.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device.

The colors the user picks, types or samples are processed in the popup and kept only in this extension's local storage on the device (settings, the current pair, up to 50 recent pairs, which the user can turn off and clear). Nothing is transmitted. The extension page's content security policy sets connect-src 'none', so the popup cannot make network requests. The popup's trust line says "No permissions · Colors stay on this device. No network requests".

What is stored

What is stored on your device: your settings (theme, target level, copy format, recent-pairs options), your current color pair, and your recent color pairs (up to 50; you can turn this off and clear it in Settings). Removing the extension deletes all of it.

More detail

The colors you pick, type or sample are processed in your browser and stored only in local extension storage on your device. The extension has no accounts, no analytics and makes no network requests. It requests no permissions and has no access to any website.

What is never collected or sent: the colors you check, the pages you visit, images you load, anything you copy.
