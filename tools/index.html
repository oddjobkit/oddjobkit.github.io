Tools

What each extension does, the permissions it asks for, and its privacy policy.

  • Hexpick

    First release · 1.0.0

    Contrast checker + color picker (WCAG)

    Pick two colors from your screen or an image to check WCAG 2.2 contrast. Get OKLCH values and a passing fix. No site access.

    No permissions

    Privacy policy

  • Picktext

    First release · 1.0.0

    Image to Text OCR – Copy Text from Images

    Copy text from any screenshot or image. Select an area, paste or drop a picture, edit the text, copy. Works offline.

    Permissions: activeTab, storage · Optional: clipboardWrite

    Privacy policy

  • Tabvault

    First release · 1.0.0

    Restore tabs: session saver & crash backup

    Never lose your tabs: automatic snapshots, named sessions and JSON backup. Free, offline, no page access.

    Permissions: tabs, storage, unlimitedStorage, alarms, favicon · Optional: tabGroups

    Privacy policy

  • Cookiemute

    In progress · 1.0.0

    Auto Reject Cookie Banners

    Clicks Reject on cookie banners for you and shows what it did. Never picks Accept all. Rules ship inside the extension.

    Permissions: all sites, scripting, storage

    Privacy policy

  • Clipstack

    In progress · 1.0.0

    Clipboard History: Private, Auto-Clearing

    Search what you copied. Skips passwords and card numbers, clears itself when Chrome closes, never leaves your device.

    Permissions: clipboardRead, offscreen, storage, alarms

    Privacy policy

  • Convertsafe

    In progress · 1.0.0

    HEIC to JPG Converter: WebP, AVIF to JPG/PNG

    Convert HEIC, WebP and AVIF to JPG, PNG or WebP on your device. Batch ZIP or folder. No upload, no limits, no sign-in.

    No permissions

    Privacy policy

  • Cleanjson

    In progress · 1.0.0

    JSON Formatter & Viewer (Local)

    Formats JSON as a searchable tree. Starts with zero site access: click to format, or allow only the sites you choose.

    Permissions: activeTab, scripting, storage · Optional: all sites

    Privacy policy

  • Localshot

    In progress · 1.0.0

    Remove EXIF Data & GPS from Photos

    Strip GPS location, camera details and hidden metadata from JPG, PNG and WebP photos. Pixels stay untouched. Nothing is uploaded.

    Permissions: storage

    Privacy policy

  • Captionlocal

    In progress · 1.0.0

    Subtitle Editor for SRT & VTT Files

    Edit, re-time, split and merge SRT and VTT subtitles beside your video. Runs offline in your browser. No upload, no watermark.

    Permissions: storage

    Privacy policy
